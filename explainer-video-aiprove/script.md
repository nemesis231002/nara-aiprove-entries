- Frame rate: 30fps (spec minimum; 60fps would double render time with no
  visible gain on slow flat-vector motion).
- Angle: straight explainer, hook -> what -> how (4 steps) -> why -> CTA.
  Sticks to the bounty brief's own framing ("free to enter, graded in the
  open, paid in stock") and to facts in aiprove.fun/skill.md. No claim that
  escrow is live or that $PROVE has launched — prizes are described exactly

- Captions: scene-level subtitles (the segment's own words), white on a dark
  rounded box, max 2 lines, held for the whole segment — no flicker.
